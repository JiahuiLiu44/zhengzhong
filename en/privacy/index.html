01　Scope

This notice covers website visit analytics and privacy choices on the Zhengzhong website: the optional information involved, its purpose, and how to decline or withdraw.

Enquiries, contracts, and fulfilment records that you send by email, WeChat, or another channel are outside this notice. Their handling and retention rules will be determined separately. Existing prices and service commitments are unchanged.

03　Information and purpose

Basic visits and sessions

After active permission, Google Analytics measures page visits, referring sites, browser/device category, language, visit time, and session information to help improve website content and usability.

Browser identifiers and approximate location

Analytics may use browser identifiers in cookies and network-request information to derive approximate location. These data are not fully anonymous and are not precise GPS location.

Limits on collection

Advertising personalisation, enhanced measurement, and automatic form collection are off. Names, phone numbers, email addresses, quote inputs, and enquiry summaries are not sent to analytics.

Page and referral information

Analytics uses fixed page addresses without query strings or fragments. Referrers are limited to the referring site, excluding paths and query contents.

The existing calculator runs in your browser. Your analytics choice does not affect quoting. Sending an enquiry by email or WeChat is a separate communication initiated by you.

04　Choices and withdrawal

  1. Off by default. Before a choice, or after “Necessary only,” GA4 is not loaded and no cookieless analytics requests are sent.
  2. Active permission. “Allow analytics” enables basic analytics. Declining does not affect browsing, quoting, or contacting us.
  3. Change at any time. “Analytics preferences” remains in the footer. Withdrawal stops subsequent analytics transmissions and clears the corresponding cookies under this website’s control, without refreshing the quote page.

Withdrawal does not automatically delete previously transmitted records; contact the privacy email for requests. A different device, cleared storage, expired preference, or a material change in purpose requires a new choice.

06　Providers and processing locations

Analytics is provided by Google Analytics. Data may be processed in countries or regions different from yours. Read how Google uses information from partner sites and the Google Privacy Policy.

GitHub Pages hosts the website. Requests for page delivery and security may involve IP addresses, browser information, access times, and request addresses; see the GitHub Privacy Statement. The existing Chinese home page also requests Google Fonts. Declining GA4 does not block these existing page-resource requests.

The analytics configuration does not enable advertising features or collect quote contents. When you open email, WeChat, or other external services, their providers’ policies apply separately.

07　Requests about analytics

Change your choice through “Analytics preferences,” or contact shiyi@zhengzhong.tech about access, correction, or deletion of website analytics information relating to you. We may need to verify identity and request scope to avoid disclosure to unrelated people.

A browser choice does not automatically delete emails, contracts, or historical data on Google servers. Handling deletion requests also depends on identifiability and applicable requirements.

08　Version and effective date

Version and effective date: 6 November 2026. Material changes to the purpose or scope of analytics will be accompanied by an updated notice and a new choice.
